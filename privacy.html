Privacy notice

Medal Drive Auto Upload is a personal utility that runs on the account owner's Windows computer.

Data used

The utility reads video files in the configured Medal capture folders, along with their filenames, sizes, modification times, and checksums. With the owner's authorization, it uploads those videos directly to the owner's Google Drive.

Google access

The utility requests Google Drive's drive.file permission, which covers specific files used with the app. It uses that access to create its archive folder, upload videos, and read file metadata to verify uploads. The utility does not require access to email, contacts, or calendars.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials, upload records, and diagnostic logs are stored locally in a restricted application folder. Archived videos remain in the owner's Google Drive, subject to the owner's Drive permissions and Google's service policies. The utility has no developer-operated server and does not send recordings or Google access tokens to this website, advertisers, or analytics services.

Retention and control

When immediate cleanup is enabled, local recordings are removed only after successful cloud verification. Cloud copies remain until the owner removes them. The owner can pause the Windows task, uninstall the utility, or revoke its access in Google Account settings. Revoking access does not itself delete archived videos. Local logs and configuration can be removed with the application after it is stopped.

This website

This static information site has no forms, analytics scripts, or embedded media. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information under its own privacy policy.
