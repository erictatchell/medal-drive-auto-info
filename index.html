Medal Drive Auto Upload

A personal Windows utility that archives completed Medal video recordings in the account owner's Google Drive.

The utility waits for recording to finish, uploads the file, verifies its size and checksum, and removes the local copy only when that cleanup option is enabled. Upload failures leave the local file in place.

This independent utility uses rclone and the Google Drive API. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Medal or Google.
